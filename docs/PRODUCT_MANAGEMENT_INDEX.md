## Product Manager Role

### Role Definition
**Location:** [team/ROLES.md](team/ROLES.md)

The Product Manager serves as the primary interface between stakeholders and the development team, responsible for:

1. ✅ Define and prioritize feature roadmap
2. ✅ Gather user requirements and feedback
3. ✅ Coordinate between stakeholders and development team
4. ✅ Manage project timeline and milestones
5. ✅ Ensure business requirements are met
6. ✅ User documentation and marketing materials

**Status:** Role defined and documented
